ABOUT REFILLPREDICT

Make the next refill easier to plan for.

RefillPredict helps people managing prescriptions and their caregivers explore how recent prices may shape an upcoming refill estimate, in clear language and with the limits made plain.

Explore features Contact the project
OUR MISSION

Make price history easier to understand.

We built this project to turn a few recent medication prices into a useful planning estimate, without presenting an estimate as a promise or a medical recommendation.

OUR VISION

More context before refill day.

We want cost-planning tools to explain where their estimates come from and where they fall short, so people can ask better questions and plan with more context.

HOW IT STARTED

A practical question, explored with data.

RefillPredict began as a software and machine-learning project exploring whether a small amount of price history could make refill planning more understandable. The work focused on a straightforward question: how might recent quarterly prices help someone think about an upcoming refill? The result is a demonstration tool for exploring that idea, not a pharmacy or healthcare service.

WHAT GUIDES THE PROJECT

Useful by being clear and careful.

Plain language

Explain the estimate in terms people can understand, not just model scores.

Transparency

Make the data and assumptions behind a result visible wherever possible.

Responsible use

Support planning without replacing a pharmacy quote or advice from a pharmacist.

A NOTE ON THE DATA

Know what the estimate is based on.

Demonstration data
The app uses seeded sample pricing data; it does not connect to live pharmacies, inventory, or price feeds.

Model-based estimates
Forecasts are produced by regression models evaluated against historical sample data. They are indicative, not guaranteed.

Not medical advice
RefillPredict is a software project, not a pharmacy, healthcare provider, or substitute for speaking with a pharmacist.
