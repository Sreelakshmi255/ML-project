ABOUT THE PROJECT

Machine learning for clearer refill-cost planning.

The project applies supervised regression to a practical pharmaceutical-economics problem: forecasting expected out-of-pocket refill costs from structured medication and historical pricing features.

Problem statement

Prescription prices can change with supply conditions, inflation, demand and manufacturer policies. Unexpected price increases may create financial pressure for people managing long-term prescriptions.

Objective

The system evaluates regression approaches capable of forecasting a future refill cost from drug text, dosage strength, brand/generic status and quarterly pricing history.

Feature engineering

  • TF-IDF representation of drug names
  • Normalized dosage strength
  • Brand versus generic encoding
  • Historical mean and latest price
  • Four-quarter price trend
  • Historical price volatility

Responsible use

The result is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed pharmacy price, medical recommendation or substitute for speaking with a pharmacist.
